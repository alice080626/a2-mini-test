Life in a Busy City

Maya moved to Brighton three years ago because she wanted to study and experience life in a new city. At first, she found the city noisy and confusing, but she soon learned how to get around. She usually travels by bus, although she sometimes cycles when the weather is good. Her favourite place is the waterfront, where she meets friends after class. There are cafés, small shops and a large park there.

Maya works part-time at a hotel. She starts work at five o'clock in the afternoon and usually finishes at nine. She enjoys talking to visitors from different countries. One of her colleagues is a concierge who often gives tourists advice about places to visit. Maya is saving money because she is going to travel around Europe next summer.

At weekends, Maya likes exploring different parts of Brighton. She says the best thing about city life is that there is always something to do. However, she sometimes misses her quiet hometown and her family.
